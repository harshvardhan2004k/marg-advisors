Suggested setup

Use a simple, compliant structure

The best beginner setup is a static site with Home, About, Services, FAQ, and Contact pages. Keep it read-only, with no login, no database, and no paid plugins.

1. Add the actual ARN or SEBI registration details.
2. Add office address, business hours, and contact info.
3. Add the risk disclaimer and service scope.
4. Publish to free hosting later when ready.
